Workforce Analytics · Power BI

Workforce Intelligence & Organizational Performance Analysis

Capstone project — Roblox Africa Operations · Tools: Excel · SQL · Power BI
Business Problem

Roblox Africa Operations held workforce data — HR, finance, health, education and performance — across 7 siloed systems covering 30,004 employees, with no unified view for leadership to assess organisational performance.

Approach
  • Integrated the 7 datasets into a single star-schema analytical model, joined on employee_id.
  • Designed the SQL database and queries — joins, aggregates, department and compensation views.
  • Built an interactive Power BI executive dashboard with drill-down filters by year, status, department, position and gender.
Key Findings

HR delivered the highest revenue-to-cost ratio (1.80) of any department, while Marketing recorded the lowest performance score (3.50). 19,990 of 30,000 insurance policies were flagged Pending or Expired.

Impact

Findings were translated into 5 evidence-based recommendations on leave capacity planning, insurance backlog resolution and underperforming-department support, presented to a simulated executive leadership audience.

Sales Analytics · Power BI

Tasty Bites Restaurant Sales Analysis

Tools: Excel · MySQL · Power BI
Business Problem

Tasty Bites had 8 months of daily sales records (Jul 2024 – Feb 2025) but no structured view of what was actually driving — or dragging on — revenue: traffic, promotions, weather or menu performance.

Approach
  • Consolidated and cleaned 250 daily sales records spanning the full trading window.
  • Modelled and queried the dataset in MySQL, then built Power BI dashboards visualising monthly sales trends, customer-feedback correlations and day-of-week traffic patterns.
Key Findings

Customer traffic was the primary revenue driver — sales grew 31% from July to October. Active promotions lifted average daily sales from GH¢5.2K to GH¢5.7K, and rainy-day sales outperformed sunny days by 18%.

Impact

Delivered 3 priority recommendations — menu bundling around the top-selling dish, weekend staffing optimisation and low-season promotions — to support sustainable revenue growth.
